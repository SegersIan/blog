## Parking Lot / GIST

Think of it as a stack. Cynefin tells you what type of problem you're facing and how to approach it. Systems thinking reveals the dynamics keeping it alive. Theory of Constraints identifies where to focus. Team Topologies checks whether the structure supports the desired flow. Wardley Mapping checks whether you're working on the right things. VSM optimizes the specific flow once everything else is aligned. And ONA shows you the hidden human wiring underneath all of it.
You don't need all of them. But knowing they exist means you pick the right lens for the situation instead of using the same hammer for everything.

The bias check here: people tend to learn one or two of these deeply and then see every problem through that lens. The real skill isn't mastering any single one — it's pattern-matching which situation calls for which lens.
